Learning guide: Patterns and evidence
Learning objective: recognise a numerical pattern and justify a prediction.
Sequence: 3, 7, 11, 15. The difference is 4. The next term is 19.
To find term n, use 4n - 1. Check n=1: 4(1)-1=3.
Practice: Find terms 6 and 10. Answers: 23 and 39.
